Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about commissions and how I work

How do I pay for a commission?

I accept PayPal (international) and PIX (Brazil). After we talk about your commission, I’ll ask for your email to send a PayPal invoice safely and quickly.

How long does a commission take?

The delivery time depends on your position on my waitlist, the complexity of your character, and the type of piece (YCH or custom). There is already an estimated timeframe shown in the table above, but it may vary depending on your idea.

Can I request changes to my commission?

I can make small adjustments (colors, markings, small fixes). Large changes (like pose, composition or major redesign) are not included and may require an additional fee.

How much do extra characters cost?

Each additional character costs the same value as a full character for the chosen style (Icon, Bust, Half-Body, etc.). The final price may increase depending on interaction and complexity between characters.

What if I don't know which style to choose?

No worries! You can send me your idea and I’ll help you choose the best option for your budget and vision. You can also check the Art Types page for visual examples.

Are there subjects you don't draw?

Yes. I do not draw extreme gore, heavy fetishes, offensive or hateful themes, or NSFW/adult content. For full details, please check the “What I Don’t Draw” section above.
